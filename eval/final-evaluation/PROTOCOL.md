# Frozen execution protocol: reviewer guide

This is a publication guide to the frozen protocol, not a replacement protocol or authorization for new model calls. Original protocol SHA-256: `6904436cf2715f30153c5639de6a9a5bf57decee181f52aeaee3aabfe7c6266c`. All original identities are in [frozen-receipts.json](results/frozen-receipts.json).

## Method and execution

StudyEval `studyeval-v1.5-micro-closure` is permanently frozen. Its eight [runtime modules](method/src/) and [self-hashed original freeze](method/original-freeze.json) are copied unchanged. The method combines artifact audit with independent correctness checks, quoted scenario preparation and blind current-question solving where applicable, independent grading checks, evidence contracts and certification dependencies. Semantic model observations are validated and reduced locally into 2/1/0/U; invalid execution is separate. There is no weighted total quality score.

Current author keys are hidden from independent solving. Learner answers, author keys, criteria and actual product consequences are distinct fields. Prior exposure consists only of earlier displayed material in the recorded action sequence. A reference answer present in an evaluator packet does not itself establish learner exposure. Expected bands and constructor rationales are never appended to evaluator or human packets.

The product and evaluator both use Hy3. Separate model calls are not external truth or independent model weights. The final adapter is `final-evaluation-adapter-2026-09-12-wire-v2`; it changes transport/storage and evidence binding, not the original rubric, prompts, reference contracts, aggregation, semantic retries or format-repair rules. Returned model aliases are recorded; immutable provider weights were not independently verified.

## Natural product campaign

The evaluated product commit is `0b23459adb7d82758f5d0df4192648d28faed0e0`. Twenty fresh courses ran through compiled production services in isolated SQLite databases with three workers: 16 original-depth courses over the 16 roots, plus four paired basic-depth courses over already counted roots. Paired courses do not increase source diversity.

Each course imports its source, confirms material role and learner contract, prepares/accepts curriculum, derives the real plan and follows actual next actions. The ceiling is two teaching portions and one naturally admitted Formal opportunity. No grades, progress or authority rows are planted. A first contextual Tutor question and one deliberately wrong Practice answer exercise diagnosis, repair and two Retests when available. Other guided/Practice/Retest selections use authored keys to traverse the workflow. This is scripted execution, not human learning.

One explicit learner retry is allowed after an offered Lesson `retry_preparation`; product-internal bounded repair remains unchanged. First failures, rejected candidates and unavailable routes stay in the denominator. There is no retry-until-pass and no substitution of a later favorable artifact for the first sampled surface.

For a naturally admitted Formal question, the exact pre-answer database is branched. An independently preregistered insufficient answer is submitted in the branch; the preregistered source summary is submitted in the main course. A "positive" candidate is not assumed sufficient for the generated question. The same main submission is replayed to check idempotency; this is separate from independent evaluator repetitions.

## Sampling and repeated judgments

Nine natural record opportunities are fixed per course: curriculum (Q2); first delivered teaching (Q1/Q3); first Tutor (Q1/Q3); Practice (Q4); repair (Q6); Retest (Q4); first admitted Formal question (Q4); main and insufficient grading branches (Q5 each). Record actual missing surfaces and reasons. Exact source, output, author-key, learner-answer and criterion bindings are frozen before final judge calls.

The repeat policy requests three observations each for 12 strong/poor controls, six attacks, six boundary cases and 12 natural slots. A natural slot uses first Tutor, then first teaching, then curriculum from that same registered source, or remains missing. Thirty-five inputs were available, producing 70 additional observations; each replicate used fresh judge requests without a response cache. Exact repeated categories include U and INVALID. Saved-response replay is a separate computation.

Human selection was fixed before final machine scores: three packets containing 27 items / 34 requested dimensions after registered fallbacks. Three genuine annotators are assigned overlapping packets to provide two independent responses per item. Annotation is still in progress. Both completed independent model reviews read only the supplied packet contents, instructions and rubric, according to their frozen provenance statements; neither is human ground truth. Their names are reviewer labels, not verified immutable model revisions.

## Accounting and reproduction

Final judge accounting includes 847 physical requests; the separate product campaign includes 380. Owner-specified rates are CNY 1/million uncached input tokens, 0.25/million cached input, and 4/million completion tokens. Reasoning tokens are already part of completion tokens and are counted once. Unknown usage is reported rather than charged zero. Percentiles use the sorted elapsed times at index `floor(p × n)`, capped at the final index.

All 279 scheduled observations finished. The frozen campaign also completed a network-disabled replay from captured wire responses. Publication checks source files and saved outputs without rerunning the evaluator, product campaign or any model. The public [verification command](README.md#reproduce-the-published-statistics) recalculates row statistics and accounting and checks the available exact inputs. Full raw-wire replay and the embargoed inputs are not publicly reproducible in this interim release.

After human answers are frozen, integrate genuine annotations with their provenance, retain U/abstentions and disagreement denominators, and publish the remaining privacy-reviewed evidence. Machine outcomes, constructor hypotheses and individual model judgments remain unchanged by that integration.
