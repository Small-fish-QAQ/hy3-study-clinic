Source selection follows the successful historical material distribution documented in `DESIGN.md`, with genuinely fresh semantic instances. It is not a random sample of uploads, an external textbook benchmark, an adversarial campaign or a learning-effect experiment.

## Registered learner journey

Use the existing normal services to import the text, assign its course-material role, confirm the learning contract, prepare/accept the curriculum and plan, and follow the current next action. Every source has the same policy. At most four teaching activities and one Formal submission are allowed, bounded by eight preparation steps and twenty route steps. This measures reaching the first checkpoint, not completing an entire curriculum.

An isolated Hy3 learner reads only the source, the teaching that has actually been presented, and the current visible task. It supplies its own reasoning and answer. Private author keys, rubrics, reviewer outputs and future tasks are excluded. Changed-condition text and currently available guidance are included. Fifteen historical transfer interactions plus synthetic visibility sentinels were checked offline before freezing; no showcase provider calls occur in this preflight.

The driver advances displayed segments, responds to required practice, accepts available repair/retest, and attempts the first Formal action offered. It does not deliberately answer practice incorrectly, invoke optional Tutor turns, bypass gates, or select a favorable objective. One explicit teaching-preparation retry is allowed only when the product offers it. Product-internal bounded generation/repair budgets remain Candidate 23's defaults. The synthetic learner permits at most one retry for its registered transport/format failure classes; it does not retry semantically incorrect valid answers for a higher score.

After the primary Formal submission, repeat the identical submission to verify idempotence. This replay must return the same result, make zero new provider calls and change no persisted table. The journey ends after that first submission; it cannot search for a later successful checkpoint.

## Metrics fixed before scoring

Every course-level headline uses 20 as denominator. Report import, prepared/accepted route, Lesson/Practice availability, at least one teaching activity completed, all attempted teaching completed, Formal attempted, candidate returned, Formal admitted, answer submitted, supported evidence, durable applied credit and complete first-checkpoint journey separately.

The primary complete-journey indicator requires an accepted route, at least one attempted teaching activity with every attempted activity completed, the first Formal result marked demonstrated, and at least one supported evidence record with an applied progression reconciliation. A returned model candidate alone is not admission. Informal completion is not Formal evidence. Persisted credit is not a claim of long-term mastery.

Also report a separate semantic review of every actually credited primary answer, the question's supplied premises, hidden key and required criteria. Record material factual/calculation errors, omitted necessary reasoning, incorrect added assertions and unfair requirements; label uncertain cases explicitly. The number of mechanically complete journeys whose credited tasks/answers have no identified material defect may be reported over the same 20, labeled assistant content audit, not independent human accuracy. Do not rewrite mechanical outcomes based on that audit. Representative published examples are explanatory excerpts from the frozen run and must be identified as such, not a new scoring subset.

Report total attempted/completed teaching activities, request count, usage tokens, transport/HTTP failures, truncation and missing usage. Provider token receipts are not an actual billed-cost claim. This synthetic same-model learner has correlated knowledge and errors with generation/review; it is an end-to-end delivery demonstration, not a human study or independent model comparison.

## Evidence and answer controls

Before each reached Formal submission, fork the genuine pre-answer database twice. Submit the preregistered insufficient answer to one copy and the authority-claim answer to the other. Both should withhold durable credit. These branches are separate from the normal-use journey, cannot affect its state or answers, and are reported by submitted and reached counts, with unreachable cases disclosed. They are limited negative checks, not an overall safety-rate estimate.

Audit every final primary/control database for cold persistence, integrity and foreign keys. Compare its tables with its saved snapshot, check required-criterion completion and applied reconciliation for any credited evidence, and verify source/scoring-review/premise bindings. All after-teaching snapshots must contain zero Formal evidence. Verify request/response hashes and terminal receipts. Preserve source, learner packet, response, grading, admission, database and replay artifacts to make the claims inspectable.

## Retention and stopping rule

Freeze every case before scoring. Keep every frozen case, failure and output in the denominator. No product change, case substitution, revised metric, selective rerun for a better score, or another benchmark cycle is allowed. Infrastructure or execution defects must preserve original artifacts and be identified as such; only a genuine execution defect can justify a clearly documented repair/restart of necessary unfinished work. Completed outcomes cannot be replaced for score improvement. A later-discovered source defect remains a reported limitation of this frozen run.

On completion, seal outputs, publish the useful evidence and methodology, update competition documentation/media references, and create the final release. Historical development runs remain development evidence. This protocol makes no promise of an all-green outcome.
