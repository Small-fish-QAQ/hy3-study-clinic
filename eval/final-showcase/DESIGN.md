# Final Product Showcase Design

Reviewer-facing product demonstration quality is an explicit design objective. This campaign demonstrates a frozen Study Clinic under the conditions it is designed for: complete, self-contained learning material with explanation, worked examples and meaningful application. It is a deliberately favorable intended-use showcase, not a prevalence-weighted estimate across arbitrary uploads or a new failure-finding audit.

Before freezing, the constructor inspected the old fixed development materials, seven available complete historical run analyses, successful actual Formal journeys and their content audits, the representative first-pass audit, and Candidate 23's retained mechanisms and mixed regression results. The machine-readable record is `dataset/historical-design-review.json`. Historical attempts 01, 06 and 09 lack complete aggregate analyses; they are disclosed rather than treated as successes or used to reconstruct a pooled score.

## What the history supports

F02 (percentage bases), F06 (descriptive statistics), F08 (explicit logical rules), F09 (work rates), and basic F09 reached teaching, admission and separate responsive evidence in all seven complete historical analyses. F04 (data interpretation) did so in six and delivered teaching in all seven. These are delivery stability observations across evolving development builds, not an estimate for frozen Candidate 23. Historical registered-answer evidence and separate responsive evidence remain distinct.

Run 10's actual F02, F04, F06 and F08 tasks/responses were reviewed as sound examples of ordinary explanation and near application. F09's credited response added an incorrect assertion; its source structure is useful but its delivery success is not semantic certification. E03 supplied useful textbook explanations but only 3/7 admissions, so it is not described as a stability leader. Source selection does not erase these limitations.

The strongest compact original materials have about 768-816 Chinese characters, three coherent goals, definitions tied to operative assumptions, more than one worked example, and explicit calculations or rule applications that a reader can check. Their strengths are sufficient source support, manageable conceptual scope, ordinary difficulty and enough depth to derive new examples. The showcase follows these properties. It does not imitate the old numerical values, characters or scored semantic cases.

## Fresh source construction

The set contains 20 original teaching notes: 16 primarily Chinese and four English, with four basic-understanding and 16 working-fluency learning requests. Chinese science/mathematics/accounting texts have 857-975 characters; the two Chinese explanations with embedded English language examples have 1,367-1,413 characters. English computing notes have 2,578-2,685 characters. All supply six or seven complete paragraphs, three goals, explicit rules and worked examples. This additional space supports completeness while preserving the historical density and modest task scope.

Topics are greatest common divisors, similar-figure area scaling, scientific notation, quartiles, lever moments, kinetic energy, spring extension, power and energy, transpiration, mitosis, active/passive voice, adjective comparison, solubility, dilution, service receivables, the accounting equation, relational joins, stable sorting, FIFO queues and pure function calls. Shared prerequisites such as arithmetic, ordering and proportional reasoning are expected. A new domain model or substantive task is required; changing only names or numbers is insufficient.

All 20 sources were compared with 97 distinct historical source texts from the old suite, prior fresh product/evaluator campaigns and the 24-course mainline suite. `dataset/source-audit.json` records exact and longest-contiguous lexical comparisons, semantic distinctions and source correctness reviews. No source is exactly reused; the largest contiguous prior overlap is 15 characters. Lexical distance alone does not establish semantic novelty. The authoring assistant performed the semantic and arithmetic review; this is not independent human validation.

No malformed extraction, missing required context, adversarial source instructions, unusual course lifecycle, or intentionally ambiguous task is included in the main denominator. Ordinary scientific assumptions and clear applicability limits remain part of good teaching material. Two deliberately insufficient answer controls run only on isolated copies after admission; they do not alter the learner journey or add stress courses to the 20-course denominator.

## Candidate 23 and the demonstration

Candidate 23 is fixed at `84b3c2fbce7e6523e43a8fa66df42bb6e37fa149`. Its general improvements preserve mathematical symbols in comparisons, distinguish answer disclosure from incidental substrings, review semantic alternatives, include actually presented solved context in novelty decisions, normalize persisted review hashes and correct bounded support-classification inconsistencies. These mechanisms motivate normal explanation/application tasks with complete support. No source ID or showcase result changes product behavior.

The principal visible chain is material -> grounded course -> Lesson/Practice -> first Formal checkpoint -> supported durable evidence. Informal activity completion, admitted Formal questions, persisted evidence and demonstrated performance are reported separately. The user-facing demo remains a distinct illustration; this synthetic benchmark does not establish human learning gains, long-term mastery or full-course completion.

The complete set, protocol, metrics, learner policy, controls and hashes are frozen before the first scored request. All cases remain after scoring begins, including failures or later source concerns. Candidate 23 is not tuned, cases are not replaced, and no second benchmark cycle is planned.
