# Deep-transfer completion

Current courses schedule a Lesson and Practice, an independent Formal checkpoint,
and a **unit transfer** assessment for each objective with current Formal authority.
Transfer follows its checkpoint. It works for a single LearningUnit and does not
require a cross-unit synthesis group.

The learner constructs a new, concrete scenario, uses the source-supported
principle to explain a judgment, then changes a decisive condition and explains
the resulting difference or the source's limits. The source rubric and four
local performance criteria are graded separately. Repeating a definition or
renaming a taught example cannot satisfy this task. Previous presented examples,
Practice/Retest prompts, and transfer responses inform the novelty judgment.

The accepted `unit_transfer` Plan item, immutable question/task, and grade carry
the same obligation. All four performance results must pass, alongside the
ordinary source criteria, before transfer evidence is eligible. Missing results
fail closed. Failure uses the existing Repair and fresh verification path;
Practice and Tutor never award evidence. Reload uses the existing accepted
assessment, immutable attempt, and separately retryable progression records.

Completion policy v3 retains the deep-transfer evidence minimum and score
threshold, and requires both ordinary Formal evidence and passed transfer for
every blocking objective. Other depths retain their existing thresholds.
The evidence representation stays bounded by the objective's independently
supported construct: identify → recognition, explain → explanation, apply →
transfer. A synthesis route label alone does not raise the mastery demand.
Durable mastery still requires representation diversity, application demand,
and delayed unseen Review evidence.

Source-only or otherwise teaching-only objectives do not gain Formal authority
from selecting a deeper course. Historical accepted plans lacking checkpoints
or current transfer items are not migrated; create a new course from the normal
material-upload and structure-acceptance flow. Existing evidence is not rewritten
or carried into the new Plan. Legacy grouped synthesis remains a separate path.

FakeProvider checks deterministic workflow markers only. Model grading quality,
including whether a scenario demonstrates meaningful transfer, belongs in
StudyEval and must not be inferred from Fake workflow tests.
