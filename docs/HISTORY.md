# Hy3 Study Clinic — Project History

This document is project history, not a second product specification. Current truth lives
in the [README](../README.md) and [current architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Stage 1 — Earlier competition submission

The project’s earlier Rhino-Bird Issue #4 submission was recorded in the immutable
`issue-4-final` tag (historical checkpoint, 2026-07).

## Stage 2 — Reliability and extraction hardening

The product then strengthened activity launchability, atomic and stale-safe grading,
section-aware additive extraction, structural mapping, lesson provenance, Fake/Hy3
provider parity, and deterministic evaluation coverage.

## Stage 3 — Dogfood exposed the orchestration gap

Real study use showed that a reliable collection of learning tools still required the
learner to reconstruct the course, decide what was next, maintain time and completion
rules, remember what had been verified, schedule synthesis and review, and recover when
the route drifted. The remaining problem was durable course execution.

## Stage 4 — Mixed-initiative Learning Execution Agent

The mixed-initiative, user-governed, evidence-gated Learning Execution Agent is now
implemented. It adds a learner-confirmed Learning Contract, learner-visible Curriculum,
accepted versioned StudyPlan, flexible SessionAgenda, conversational StudySession, formal
progression, Coverage/Risk Ledger, and advisory adversarial readiness while preserving
existing grounding, grading, learner-state, and history guarantees.

## Stage 5 — Source authority and semantic support

The source-authority boundary was made explicit: claim class and scope origin are
separate; Hy3 emits a blind `subjectDependency` attestation plus one relation per offered
candidate and bounded support groups; local code derives coverage, binding match,
mis-binding, contradiction, and the final verdict. Accepted artifacts are re-derived at
consequential boundaries and stale or mismatched results fail closed.

## Stage 6 — Competition evaluation preparation

The project now publishes the StudyEval method specification for open-ended educational
quality. The executable harness, frozen corpus, and result tables remain scheduled work;
the specification does not claim experiments that have not run.

Older audits, implementation plans, and design drafts remain available through Git
history. The demoted [historical design](history/AGENT_PRODUCT_DESIGN.md) is context only;
the current architecture document is the authority for implemented behavior.
